# Risk register

Each risk is source-supported (Build Plan) unless marked *raised during setup*. **Probability and
impact below are proposed starting values for the project lead to confirm**, not assessments by the
team. Owners are TODO until the project lead assigns them. Review weekly at the gate.

Scale: Probability and Impact = High / Medium / Low. Status: Open / Mitigating / Closed.

| ID | Risk | Probability | Impact | Owner | Mitigation | Trigger | Status | Target resolution |
| -- | ---- | ----------- | ------ | ----- | ---------- | ------- | ------ | ----------------- |
| R-01 | **SIM key readiness.** Until the core holds Ki and OPc for real SIMs, no real phone can attach; the same keys decide whether existing customers can move across later. | High | High | TODO | Settle SIM keys with the SIM supplier and network team; keep working against the lab core and mocks meanwhile; first real-SIM attach must use a line created through RatelLink. | No keys by Fri Oct 9, or keys delivered in a form RatelLink cannot import | Open | Oct 9 (Week 2 gate) |
| R-02 | **VoLTE phone compatibility.** Many Android phones enable VoLTE only for listed networks; some need voice settings on the SIM or IPsec to the P-CSCF. | High | High | Network team (TODO name) | Collect at least six test phone models; find out which work in the first two weeks; ask the SIM supplier whether SIMs support VoLTE. | By Oct 16 no sellable phone registers and calls reliably | Open | Oct 16 (voice decision point) |
| R-03 | **RatelMeter counter accuracy.** Counters must match the interface within 1%; the UPF counter mechanism may lose bytes under thousands of short flows. | Medium | High | TODO | Spike nftables per-element counters, per-line accounting rules and conntrack; measure, do not assume; accuracy check on first real traffic. | Totals differ from interface counters by more than 1% | Open | Oct 16 (Week 3 gate) |
| R-04 | **Payment and webhook correctness.** A webhook delivered twice, or a forged one, could credit wrongly; one gateway, one chance. | Medium | High | TODO | Verify signature, re-query the provider, `provider_ref` unique; replay tests; sandbox end to end; two reviewers; security review in Week 6. | Any double credit in test, or a webhook accepted without verification | Open | Oct 30 (sandbox end to end), Nov 6 (review) |
| R-05 | **Live-change uncertainty.** Whether Open5GS v2.8.0 can throttle or detach a live session within 60 seconds is unknown. Without it, suspended or exhausted lines keep browsing and calling until reattach. | Medium | High | TODO | Time-boxed spike answering the three questions in writing in Week 2; no live-change code until answered; fallback "changes at next attach" written down and told to customer care. | Spike shows a target cannot be met in time | Open | Oct 9 (write-up), Oct 30 (build if small) |
| R-06 | **Restore and recovery readiness.** Backups that were never restored are not backups. | Medium | High | Network team (TODO name) | Backups of MongoDB, PostgreSQL and MySQL and a real restore rehearsal in Week 6; Ki/OPc never leave core-cp unencrypted. Open5GS keeps its own plaintext copy in the `open5gs` database, so any backup containing it must be encrypted before it leaves core-cp (ADR 0006). | Restore not performed by end of Week 6 | Open | Nov 6 (Week 6 gate) |
| R-07 | **Replay and idempotency correctness.** Double-counted usage or calls, double-credited payments or vouchers, and re-rated records erode customer trust faster than an outage. | Medium | High | TODO | Keys on `imsi + period_start` and `call_id`; upsert; rating marks records rated in the same transaction; `Idempotency-Key`; replay and restart tests in Week 6. | Any replay test changes a total | Open | Nov 6 (Week 6 gate) |
| R-08 | **Delivery bottleneck: limited backend capacity.** RatelLink, RatelMeter, BSS lines and BSS money are all backend-heavy; with fewer than four backend developers workstreams serialize, and RatelLink needs the most careful developer, not the fastest. | High | High | Project lead | Assess team before assigning; follow the Build Plan's team splits; RatelLink first, then a thin slice of BSS lines; contracts and mocks so nobody waits; keep scope to the demo. | A weekly gate slips by more than a day, or one person is the reviewer for everything | Open | Reviewed weekly |
| R-09 | **MongoDB authentication.** MongoDB holds every line's Ki and OPc and runs without authentication by default; nothing may go near real SIMs until authentication is on and it is bound to 127.0.0.1. | Medium | High | Network team (TODO name) | Network-team task in Week 1; software team verifies before importing any real key. RatelLink's `sim_key` is encrypted at rest (ADR 0006), but the Open5GS database holds Ki and OPc in plaintext and the encryption key file sits on the same host, so MongoDB authentication, localhost binding and host access control still carry the weight. | Real SIM keys requested while MongoDB auth is off | Open | Before first real SIM (Oct 9) |
| R-10 | **Network team load.** One team carries RatelCore, RatelVoice, RatelOps and the radio. | Medium | Medium | Network lead | RatelVoice gets its own engineer; software team avoids unscheduled asks; agree call record fields in Week 2. | Network deliverable for a gate late | Open | Reviewed weekly |
| R-11 | **KYC and registration rules unconfirmed.** Exact NCC SIM registration requirements are compliance's call and must be confirmed before the pilot. | Medium | High | TODO (compliance contact) | Confirm requirements with compliance; no line activates until KYC verified; no invented rules. | No confirmation by start of Week 6 | Open | Nov 6 |
| R-12 | **Contract gaps** *(raised during setup).* `openapi.yaml` has `TODO(contract)` items (response bodies, field formats, `end_reason` values, cursor parameter, ingest endpoint). | R-13 | **Fixed-address plan.** Giving every line a fixed IP from `10.45.0.0/16` makes IMSI-to-IP a lookup but means sizing the pool for every line; the fallback (parsing the SMF log) breaks quietly when the format moves. | Low | Medium | TODO | Confirm the rule with RatelLink's owner on day one; keep a 24-hour no-reuse hold; size the pool. | Pool exhaustion, or an address charged to the wrong line | Open | Oct 9 |
| R-14 | **Encryption key loss or exposure** *(raised by ADR 0006, proposed values)*. The key file that decrypts RatelLink's stored Ki and OPc is on core-cp, next to MongoDB. Losing it loses every stored ciphertext. Exposure together with a copy of `ratel_link` exposes the keys. Open5GS's own plaintext copy is outside this protection. | Low | High | TODO | Owner-only key file checked at startup; separate offline backup, never stored with database backups (procedure and owner TODO); exposure steps in the RatelLink runbook; re-encryption tooling and a KMS are not built yet. | Key file missing or unreadable at startup; any backup containing both the key file and the database; any suspicion the key was copied | Open | Before the first real SIM key is imported |
